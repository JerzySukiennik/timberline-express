All audio is **real recorded sound** downloaded from the sources below. None of it is synthesized
or AI-generated. Every license was checked on the source page on 2026-09-15. Only CC0 / public
domain and CC BY 4.0 material is used. Nothing comes from the Sonniss GDC bundles.
